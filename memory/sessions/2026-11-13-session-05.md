**Initial learner response:** “I would look at the results of the evaluation, and the operational reliability of the model.”

**Tutor feedback:** “Yes—that covers the two important dimensions: Predictive quality: whether the model’s predictions remain useful. Evaluation on recent production examples needs actual outcomes or labels when available; an old test score alone doesn’t establish current quality. Operational reliability: whether the deployed service responds successfully and quickly enough, without excessive errors or downtime. For the clinic, a service can respond reliably while making poor predictions—or make good predictions but be unavailable when staff need it. Monitoring signals guide investigation; they don’t automatically mean retraining is the right fix.”
