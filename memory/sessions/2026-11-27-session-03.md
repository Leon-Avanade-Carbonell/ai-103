### Ordinary lesson checks

1. **Prompt:** 'Tutor example: A teammate creates one experiment branch, shortens the system prompt and switches to a cheaper model. Quality drops. Why is that experiment difficult to interpret, and how would you reorganize it?'

   **Initial learner response:** 'Because they made so much changes in one branch. It should have a different branches. One of the prompt, and the other for the cheaper model.'

   **Feedback:** Correct isolation decision. Tutor explained that attribution could be prompt, model, or interaction, and branches should share baseline, test requests and criteria; successful changes can later be combined. No follow-up answer.

2. **Prompt:** 'A shorter-prompt experiment fails your quality threshold. A teammate wants to delete its branch. What evidence would you preserve before deleting it, and why?'

   **Initial learner response:** 'I would try to keep the score and comments on what changed, so we can know that we have approached it that way before.'

   **Feedback:** Affirmed scores/comments and configuration rationale to avoid repeating failures; tutor supplied preserving raw responses and test/configuration details for inspection and reproduction. Deletion is allowed if results are already committed and documented. No learner follow-up confirming these additions; comprehensive preservation was not independently demonstrated.

3. **Context:** Tutor explained that merging accepted configuration and tagging does not automatically deploy. **Prompt:** 'Tutor example: The shorter prompt passes all predefined quality, cost, and response-time requirements. The cheaper model saves more money but fails the required quality threshold. Which variant would you merge, and does merging it mean the production agent has already changed?'

   **Initial learner response:** 'The shorter model. Merging it does not automatically mean it is production. there are still some deployment steps and configurations like traffic management.'

   **Feedback:** Accepted conditionally, 'assuming you mean the shorter-prompt variant'; affirmed merge/deploy separation and clarified traffic management depends on serving architecture and is not implied by Git merge. No follow-up verified the prompt/model wording; preserve the ambiguity.
